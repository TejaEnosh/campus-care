🚑 First aid basics

Handy steps while help is on the way.

🔥 Minor burn

Cool under running tap water for 10–20 minutes. Don’t apply toothpaste, butter or ice. Cover loosely with a clean cloth. Seek help for large or blistering burns.

🩸 Cut or bleeding

Wash hands, press a clean cloth firmly on the wound and raise the area. Clean gently with water once bleeding stops. Go to the health centre if it won’t stop in 10 minutes.

🤒 High fever

Rest, drink plenty of fluids, wear light clothes. Inform your warden or roommate. Visit the doctor if fever lasts over 2 days or crosses 103°F (39.4°C).

😮‍💨 Fainting

Lay the person flat and raise their legs. Loosen tight clothes and give air. If not awake in a minute, call emergency immediately.

🤢 Food poisoning

Sip ORS or clean water often, rest, eat light foods like rice or banana. Report to the mess committee. See a doctor for blood in stool or severe weakness.
